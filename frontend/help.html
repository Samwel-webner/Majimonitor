Help Center

Everything you need to know about using MajiMonitor

Quick Start Guide

1
Check the Dashboard
The Dashboard shows a live overview of every monitoring site — color-coded green (safe), yellow (warning), or red (critical) based on the latest sensor readings.
2
Click into a Site
Click any site card to see its individual parameter readings and a 24-hour history chart. Click different parameter cards to switch what the chart shows.
3
Respond to Alerts
Visit the Alerts page to see everything that's breached a safe threshold. Acknowledge an alert once you're aware of it, and Resolve it once the issue is fixed.
4
Export Reports
Both the Site Detail page and Alerts page have an "Export CSV" button, letting you download readings or alert history to open in Excel.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do the severity colors mean?
Green means the reading is within the safe range. Yellow (warning) means it's outside safe but not yet dangerous. Red (critical) means it's outside even the warning range and needs urgent attention.
Why didn't I get an alert email?
MajiMonitor only sends emails for critical alerts, to avoid flooding your inbox. Warning-level alerts are visible on the dashboard and Alerts page but don't trigger an email.
I forgot my password. What do I do?
On the login page, click "Forgot Password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link valid for 30 minutes.
Can I add a new monitoring site myself?
Only admin accounts can add or deactivate sites, via the Site Management page. Contact your administrator if you need a new site added.

How Alert Status Is Determined

Each water quality parameter has two threshold bands: a safe range and a wider warning range. A reading is classified as follows:

✓
Safe
The reading falls within the ideal safe range for that parameter.
!
Warning
The reading is outside the safe range, but still within the wider warning range — worth monitoring, not yet dangerous.
✕
Critical
The reading falls outside even the warning range and requires urgent attention.

About MajiMonitor

MajiMonitor is a real-time water quality monitoring system built for THIWASCO, tracking pH, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, conductivity, temperature, and TDS across monitoring sites along the Thika River basin. It automatically flags unsafe readings, notifies staff by email, and keeps a full historical record for reporting and compliance.
